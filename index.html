⚡

Strength First

Every session starts heavy. Muscle is your metabolic engine — build it and fat loss follows.

🔄

Progressive Overload

Add weight, reps, or density every week. Without progressive challenge, nothing changes.

🩺

Longevity Minded

At 39, recovery is training. Wednesday and weekends aren't laziness — they're when adaptation happens.

🔥

CompTrain DNA

Conditioning finishers after strength, AMRAPs and EMOMs to build engine capacity without wrecking recovery.

🎯

Marchon Influence

Physique-driven programming. Accessory work is intentional. We care how you look, not just times.

📈

Metrics Matter

Track your lifts. Weekly photos. Weigh same day each week. Data beats feelings.

// Food & Supplement Guide
FOOD GUIDE

Quick-reference macros and your supplement stack.

🥩

Protein Sources

Chicken breast, turkey mince, lean beef, eggs, salmon, tuna, Greek yoghurt, cottage cheese, whey, casein. 30–50g per meal.

🍚

Training Carbs

White rice, oats, sweet potato, banana, pasta, fruit. Eat the bulk of carbs around training windows.

🥑

Healthy Fats

Avocado, olive oil, salmon, whole eggs, nuts (handful max). Keep fats away from pre/post workout.

🥦

Free Vegetables

Spinach, broccoli, kale, courgette, peppers, cucumber. Low calorie, high fibre — eat freely at any meal.
